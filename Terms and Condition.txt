Secondary Boarding School Terms and Conditions
1. Admission and Enrollment
Admission is subject to academic performance, behavioral history, and availability of space.

Parents/guardians must provide accurate personal, medical, and academic records.

Enrollment is confirmed only after full payment of fees and submission of required documents.

The school reserves the right to withdraw admission if false information is discovered.

2. Fees and Payments
Tuition, boarding, and other fees must be paid by the deadlines stated in the school calendar.

Late payments may attract penalties or suspension of services.

Fees are non-refundable except under exceptional circumstances approved by the school board.

Additional charges may apply for extracurricular activities, excursions, or special programs.

3. Code of Conduct
Students must respect staff, peers, and property at all times.

Bullying, harassment, violence, substance abuse, or possession of prohibited items will result in disciplinary action.

Students must wear the prescribed school uniform and maintain personal hygiene.

Use of mobile phones and electronic devices is restricted to approved times and areas.

4. Boarding Regulations
Students must comply with boarding house routines, including curfews, meal times, and study hours.

Dormitories must be kept clean and orderly; inspections will be conducted regularly.

Unauthorized absence from the boarding facility is strictly prohibited.

Visitors are allowed only with prior approval from the school administration.

5. Academic Expectations
Students are required to attend all classes, complete assignments, and participate in examinations.

Academic dishonesty (cheating, plagiarism, collusion) will result in disciplinary measures.

Continuous assessment contributes to overall performance evaluation.

Students must participate in compulsory extracurricular and enrichment programs.

6. Health and Safety
Parents/guardians must disclose any medical conditions and provide necessary medication.

The school provides basic medical care but is not liable for chronic or undisclosed conditions.

Emergency medical treatment may be sought at the discretion of the school, with costs borne by parents/guardians.

Students must comply with safety rules during sports, laboratory work, and excursions.

7. Communication
Official communication between the school and parents/guardians will be via approved channels (letters, emails, portals).

Parents/guardians must keep the school updated with current contact information.

Students are expected to communicate respectfully with staff and peers.

Confidential matters will be handled in accordance with school policy.

8. Property and Liability
Students are responsible for their personal belongings; the school is not liable for loss or theft.

Any damage to school property caused by a student will be charged to the parent/guardian.

School facilities must be used responsibly and only for approved purposes.

Unauthorized use of school equipment or property is prohibited.

9. Discipline and Expulsion
The school reserves the right to suspend or expel students for serious misconduct.

Disciplinary decisions are final and binding.

Parents/guardians will be notified promptly of disciplinary actions.

Expelled students are not entitled to refunds of fees already paid.

10. Attendance and Punctuality
Regular attendance is compulsory; absences must be justified with written communication from parents/guardians.

Repeated lateness or absenteeism may result in disciplinary action.

Students must return promptly after holidays and breaks.

11. Technology and Internet Use
Students must use school computers and internet responsibly.

Accessing inappropriate content or engaging in cyberbullying is strictly prohibited.

Misuse of technology may result in loss of privileges and disciplinary action.

12. Extracurricular Activities
Participation in sports, clubs, and cultural activities is encouraged and sometimes compulsory.

Students must adhere to rules governing extracurricular programs.

Injuries sustained during activities will be treated, but liability remains with parents/guardians.

13. Privacy and Data Protection
The school will collect and store student information for administrative and academic purposes.

Personal data will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Parents/guardians consent to the use of student images in school publications unless otherwise stated.

14. Amendments
The school reserves the right to amend these terms and conditions at any time.

Parents/guardians will be notified of changes through official communication channels.

Continued enrollment constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
