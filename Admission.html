Admission Process

  • Purchase and complete the admission form.
  • Submit required documents (birth certificate, previous school records, passport photographs).
  • Sit for the entrance examination.
  • Attend an interview with the admissions committee.
  • Receive admission offer and complete registration.

Admission Requirements

Admission Form

Completed admission form purchased from the school office or online.

Passport Photographs

Two recent passport-sized photographs of the applicant.

Birth Certificate

A valid copy of the applicant’s birth certificate.

Academic Records

School reports and transcripts from the applicant’s previous institution.
